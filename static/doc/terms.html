User Agreement

Welcome to the Lost Article Matching Platform (LAMP). By creating an account or using our services, you agree to these terms.

1. Eligibility

This platform is exclusively for the students, faculty, and staff of this campus. You must use a valid campus-related identity to register.

2. User Conduct

  • Accuracy: All lost and found reports must be truthful. Falsifying reports is strictly prohibited.
  • Content: You may not upload images or descriptions that are offensive, illegal, or violate third-party intellectual property.
  • Security: You are responsible for keeping your JWT-based credentials secure.

3. Item Recovery Process

LAMP provides matching suggestions based on AI algorithms. We do not participate in the actual exchange of items. Users are advised to meet in well-lit, public campus areas to complete returns.

4. Limitation of Liability

LAMP is provided "as-is." We are not responsible for:

  • Failure to find or match a lost item.
  • Any damage or loss occurring during the return process.
  • Technical interruptions or data loss.

5. Termination

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate campus policies or the terms listed above.
