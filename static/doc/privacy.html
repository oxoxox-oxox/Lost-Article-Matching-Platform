Privacy Policy

Your privacy is a priority at LAMP. This policy explains how we collect and use your information.

1. Information Collection

Account Information

We store your email and a securely hashed version of your password (using passlib). We do not store plain-text passwords.

Report Data

When you report an item, we collect descriptions and images. These are stored in our MySQL database and used to facilitate matching.

2. AI & Data Processing

To improve matching accuracy, we use AI services (including OpenAI and ZhipuAI). By uploading item details, you acknowledge that this data may be processed by these third-party providers to identify objects and keywords.

3. Cookies & Security

We use JSON Web Tokens (JWT) for session management. These tokens are stored on your device to keep you logged in. We implement SQLAlchemy-based security protocols to prevent unauthorized database access.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your contact information is only revealed to another user once a potential match is confirmed and both parties agree to connect.

5. Your Rights

You may view, edit, or delete your lost/found reports at any time through your account dashboard. For total account deletion, please contact the system administrator.
